# Note 7: the contest ladder, with names

Robert, 2026-11-13: "this needs names throughout the game, because the game is a constant contest of stats. It's not
binary win/loss, and you can feel when you are dominating someone."

So every contest in the game uses ONE comparison and ONE ladder of names: a hit against a body, a grab, a struggle, a
tackle, a shove, a pin, a lift, a ram, two beams meeting. The comparison is the one Plan 18 built (the force or skill that
arrives, minus what the other side can stand against). Only the inputs change per contest.

## The ladder (same seven steps everywhere)

| Score | Said from the winner's side | Said from the loser's side | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 or more | **Dominating** | **Dominated** | No contest. Finishing moves are allowed (the neck snap, the one-punch knockout, tearing free) |
| 1.5 to 3 | **Overpowering** | **Overpowered** | You win, quickly, and they can barely act |
| 0.5 to 1.5 | **Edging** | **Edged** | You are winning, but they can still turn it with skill or effort |
| -0.5 to 0.5 | **Even** | **Even** | A real fight; inputs and skill decide it. Everything inside 1.5 either way plays the browser game's own struggle; the rules change only from Overpowering outward |

A contest is never just won or lost: it sits on a step, and the step can move during the contest (a struggle that starts
Even can slide to Overpowered as stamina drains, or swing back with a skill).

## The four ways a hit can put a body down

The knock-down outcome is the same ladder applied to one hit against one body's footing:

| Name | Step it comes from | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Driven** | below 0 (Braced if guarding) | Stays up, driven back (feet slide, body leans into it) |
| **Dropped** | 0 to 1.5 (Even and Edging): a hit that matches your footing drops you | Down on the back, sits right up: the street-fight fall. Can guard, kick, scoot, shoot, grab a leg, get up when chosen |
| **Floored** | 1.5 to 3 (Overpowering) | Down hard, slides a little, then can act from the ground |
| **Laid out** | 3 or more (Dominating) | Stunned, or knocked out if the body is ordinary |

A guarded hit that holds is **Braced** (feet planted, you feel it). That is the planted rung from Plan 8.

## How you feel it (so the names are never only words)

- **A small meter by the target's name** shows the step, in the COF Tactics colours: green when you are ahead, yellow at
  Even, red when you are behind. It moves during a struggle.
- **The step changes how bodies move.** The winner's animation plays at full speed and smooth; the loser's strains,
  slows and shakes. Dominating looks effortless.
- **Camera and sound** follow it: a heavier camera kick and a deeper impact sound on the bigger steps.
- **One word at the moment it matters** ("OVERPOWERED", "DOMINATING") in the comic look, used sparingly so it means
  something.

## Where it applies

Hits and the knock-down outcome (Plans 8 and 18); grabs, chokes, holds and breaking free (Plan 18); tackles and rams,
including ramming a plane (Plans 8 and 19); lifting and throwing (whether you can lift it at all is the same comparison);
beam struggles and power clashes (Plan 10); and, later, social contests (intimidation, interrogation) can use the same
ladder.

Names are a first proposal for Robert to change. The numbers are Plan 18's thresholds (aligned 2026-11-13). The losing side mirrors them below 0: Edged, Overpowered from -1.5, Dominated from -3.
