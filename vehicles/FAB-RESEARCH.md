## Verdict

**(1) Player fighter jet + hero ramming planes.** The F-16/F-35/F-18/F-15 plugins (#1) are the only C++ flight model with replication. At $40-80, the main thing worth having is the jet sound system: engine sound by RPM and distance. Their flight model and replication are client-driven assumptions, and we would throw them away in a server-authoritative design. Battle System V2 (#5) proves a dedicated server can handle 100 AI planes, but it is Blueprint, so it is a reference, not code to keep. The ramming itself is hit detection plus impulses that we write anyway. Savings: small, mostly audio.

**(2) AI airliner traffic.** Nothing ready-made exists. The cheap Blueprint spline-follower (#6) and Flying System (#2, $4) show the pattern: fly along splines, bank in turns, slow down at triggers. Airliners on rails are simple to write ourselves in C++ and cheap on the server. Savings: very little. You could buy #2 for $4 just to study it.

**(3) Boats with buoyancy.** This is where Fab saves real work. Dynamic Real Water (#8) is C++ with source, 75 classes, a server-owned wave clock and boats with engines and sails. The hard part of networked boats is clients agreeing on wave height, and #8 already solves that. It is the one real candidate. The cheap choice is Epic's own free Buoyancy Component (#13) with the free Water Physics plugin (#10) for reference, adding our own engine and rudder forces. Fluid Flux and the Ferry Boat are not networked, so skip them.

**Version risk.** None of these list 5.9. The C++ plugins (#1, #4, #8, #9) usually just need a rebuild, but the seller may not update them. Water Physics already stops at 5.7. Only Epic's in-engine water is safe on 5.9. Before buying #8, check its 5.9 support and its Discord or changelog.
